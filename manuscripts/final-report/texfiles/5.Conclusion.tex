\section{Conclusion}

MaskedBiMamba combines bidirectional temporal modeling with pose-quality weighting, and FallGuard integrates the classifier with persistent edge execution and deferred cloud delivery. Matched comparisons showed higher precision at the common threshold and greater frame-loss tolerance than TCNTE, while TE led clean F1. Component ablations supported attention and bidirectional processing but qualified the clean-data benefits of masking and quality pooling. Hardware and recovery tests established disconnected execution and preservation of the tested records. Continuous-recording calibration and longer camera tests are needed to assess operational reliability.
