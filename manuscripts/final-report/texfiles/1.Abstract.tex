\begin{abstract}
Camera-based fall detection requires reliable decisions from incomplete pose sequences and continued operation during network interruptions. Performance on complete sequences alone does not establish either capability. Here we introduce MaskedBiMamba, a skeleton sequence classifier that combines bidirectional state-space modeling, temporal attention, frame masking, and quality-weighted pooling. Across four subject-disjoint folds and three seeds on CAUCAFall and GMDCSA-24, it achieved the highest window precision among five temporal models. Under 30\% random frame loss, its window F1 was $0.6276\pm0.0507$, compared with $0.4361\pm0.0669$ for the temporal convolution--Transformer baseline. The Transformer encoder led clean window F1, while masking and quality pooling showed mixed clean-data effects in component ablations. We integrated the classifier with Hailo-accelerated pose extraction, local alert storage, and deferred cloud delivery on a Raspberry Pi. The disconnected video pipeline processed 20.56 frames/s, and recovery tests preserved all 85 evaluated records and their original capture times. These results support combining temporal classification with persistent edge execution, while continuous-monitoring accuracy and alert calibration require further evaluation.
\end{abstract}
