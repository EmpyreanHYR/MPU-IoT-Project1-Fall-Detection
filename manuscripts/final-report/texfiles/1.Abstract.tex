\begin{abstract}
We present MaskedBiMamba and FallGuard, combining robust skeleton sequence classification with an operational edge-cloud fall-detection system. MaskedBiMamba integrates bidirectional state-space modeling, temporal attention, training-time frame masking, and quality-weighted pooling. Across four subject-disjoint folds and three seeds on CAUCAFall and GMDCSA-24, it achieved the highest window precision among five temporal models at the common decision threshold. Under 30\% random frame loss, its window F1 was $0.6276\pm0.0507$, compared with $0.4361\pm0.0669$ for the temporal convolution--Transformer baseline, retaining 98.1\% of its clean F1. Attention and bidirectional processing improved the clean-data ablations; the Transformer encoder led clean window F1. FallGuard couples the classifier with Hailo-accelerated pose extraction, persistent local alert records, and deferred cloud delivery on a Raspberry Pi. The disconnected video pipeline processed 20.56 frames/s, an 11.9-fold increase over CPU pose extraction, and recovery tests preserved all 85 evaluated records and their capture times. The project delivers a frame-loss-tolerant classifier and a working data path from camera input to local inference, cloud history, and user review.
\end{abstract}
