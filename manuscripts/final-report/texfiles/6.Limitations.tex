\subsection{Limitations}

The primary evaluation covers 14 subject groups: ten from CAUCAFall and four from GMDCSA-24. This limits the range of people and fall behaviors represented. Automatically extracted poses contain tracking and localization errors, and the window-labeling rule omits ambiguous transition boundaries. The common threshold of 0.5 was not selected to balance missed falls and false alarms in a particular monitoring setting.

External Le2i video F1 lies close to the all-positive baseline of 0.8125. This class imbalance, together with low video specificity, limits the evidence for cross-dataset discrimination. Reliable participant identities are unavailable for the Le2i evaluation, so person-independent transfer remains unverified.

The hardware experiment uses a small, balanced sample of synthetic videos. It demonstrates local execution with network access disabled and measures the implemented backends; accuracy on real continuous monitoring is untested. A live deployment also encounters changing illumination, multiple people, and camera placement. The ten-minute replay covers a short session rather than all-day camera reliability, and power consumption remains to be measured.
